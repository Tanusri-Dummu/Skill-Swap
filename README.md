# ✦ SkillSwap

### Learn a Skill. Share Your Talent. 🤝

SkillSwap is a student-focused peer-to-peer skill exchange platform designed to help students connect, share their skills, and learn new skills from each other.

## 📌 About The Project

SkillSwap provides a simple platform where students can discover skills offered by other students and connect with peers who have complementary learning goals.

Instead of traditional learning alone, SkillSwap encourages students to **teach what they know and learn what they want** through peer-to-peer skill exchange.
